Hi! I'm Pip.

I build the dreams you fall asleep inside.

Junior Dream Engineer at the Midnight Nursery. I make lullabies, nightlights and bedtime stories for the neighbourhood's little ghosts and creatures. Tickle me. Boop things. Please don't leave it quiet.

How I got here

🌙

A lullaby fell off a cloud

One very quiet night, the last note of the city's lullaby slipped out of the sky and landed in a knitted nightcap. The cap giggled. That was me.

🔧

First day at the Nursery

The Midnight Nursery hired me on the spot. Any baby who hates silence is a natural dream engineer. I got a tiny wrench and a lot of blankets.

🏮

The neighbourhood

Now I fly the rooftops with a lantern, delivering dreams to sleepy ghosts, night owls, and one grumpy teapot called Mr. Whistle.

The Lullaby Lab

My very first music box. Tap the box to wind it, or play the star keys yourself. The notes are tuned so nothing can sound wrong. I tested this very thoroughly (I fell asleep twice).

Nightlight Collection

The Crescent Sleepy lamp, in three glow settings. Tap the lamp to switch it on and the whole nursery goes to night. Tap again to wake everyone up.
